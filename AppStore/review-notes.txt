No account or sign-in is required. The game runs entirely offline — it has no
networking code, no third-party SDKs, no ads and no in-app purchases. All
progress is stored locally in UserDefaults.

Progression: only the first world (Green Garden) is unlocked at the start;
finishing a course unlocks the next world. If you want to see the later worlds
without playing through the game, the Daily Challenge on the main menu draws its
hole from all nine worlds, including the locked ones.

Controls: drag your finger anywhere on the screen away from the ball to aim —
the drag length sets the power — and release to putt. Pinch to zoom the camera.

The app supports iPhone and iPad in both portrait and landscape.
