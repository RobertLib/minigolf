# Privacy Policy — Minigolf

*Last updated: August 2, 2026*

## In short

Minigolf does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

## In detail

**We collect no data.** The app never asks for your name, email address, phone
number or any other personal information. No account and no sign-in is required.

**It does not connect to the internet.** Minigolf contains no networking code.
All content — holes, graphics and sounds — ships inside the app, and the game
works entirely offline.

**No analytics, no advertising.** The app contains no ad networks, no analytics
frameworks and no third-party libraries that transmit anything anywhere.

**No in-app purchases.** The app contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

**Where your progress is stored.** Your game progress (unlocked worlds, hole
records, collected stars, trophies, career stats and settings) is stored solely
on your device using the system UserDefaults store. This data never leaves the
device. It is included in your regular device backup (iCloud or computer) if you
have one enabled, in which case Apple's own policies apply. Deleting the app
removes this data from your device.

**Device access.** The app does not use the camera, microphone, location
services, contacts, health data or any other sensitive permission. It only uses
haptic feedback and audio playback.

**Children.** The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, no
data about children is created either.

## Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
