Real minigolf in 3D — 108 hand-designed holes across nine worlds. Drag your finger, let go, and watch the ball bounce exactly where you aimed it.

NINE WORLDS, 108 HOLES
Twelve holes per world, each with rules of its own:
• Green Garden — sunlit classics to warm up on
• Desert Oasis — sand traps, water and spinning rotors
• Jungle Temple — mud, portals and rushing rivers
• Frozen Fjord — slick ice and tilted greens
• Neon Nights — pinball bumpers and warp gates
• Volcano Forge — lava, geysers and iron gates
• Clockwork Works — turntables, pistons and cannons
• Storm Coast — gusting wind and jumps over the surf
• Orbital Station — loops, magnets and open space

A GAME THAT GROWS WITH YOU
The first hole is a metre-wide straight with one hedgehog on it. The last is ten
times the size and played in stages: a run-up, a banked corner, a loop, a gap
with nothing under it, and a ramp to a raised green. Geometry, obstacles and par
all build the whole way — world by world and hole by hole.

CONTROLS YOU GET ON THE FIRST PUTT
Drag back from the ball — the longer the drag, the harder the shot. Release to swing. A dotted aim line predicts the path including bank shots off the rails, and turns gold the moment the putt is going in. Want it harder? Shorten the line or switch it off in settings.

REAL MINIGOLF RULES
The stroke limit is par + 3. Water and lava cost a penalty stroke and send the ball back. Four lives per course — run out and it's game over. Hole-in-one, Eagle, Birdie, Bogey… and after the 108th hole, a final golfer rating from Weekend Golfer to Golf Legend.

THINGS TO COLLECT
• A hidden bonus star on every hole (108 in total)
• 1–3 stars per hole depending on your stroke count
• 14 unlockable balls — from classic white through chrome to a legend with a glowing trail
• 18 trophies that track your progress
• Career stats in the clubhouse

DAILY CHALLENGE
One hole a day, the same one for every player, drawn from all nine worlds. It keeps a daily streak, awards a medal against par, and replays as often as you like.

PRACTICE, NO PRESSURE
Play any unlocked hole on its own — no lives, no rush, just you and your personal best.
